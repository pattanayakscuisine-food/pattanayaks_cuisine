How It All Started

The Story Behind
pattanayak's cuisine

As an Indian Bengali, food is a love language for us. Since childhood, we have seen our mother cooking different kinds of tasty and delicious dishes for the family. Growing up under Mrs. Pattanayak's care, her daughter naturally developed a deep interest in cooking. What started with helping in the kitchen and preparing everyday meals soon turned into exploring different cuisines with her own hands. Inspired by her mother's magic in the kitchen and driven by her own love for food, she kept experimenting with new recipes and flavors. Slowly, her dishes started receiving great reviews from friends, colleagues, and family members. But before anyone else's opinion, there was always one review that mattered the most — her younger brother's. He was the unofficial food critic of the house. Every time she cooked something new, he would be the first one to taste it. The funny part? Instead of simply giving a review, he would immediately start requesting the next dish that came to his mind. One good meal would lead to another demand, then another, and another. As a responsible elder sister, she tried her best to fulfill as many of those requests as possible, although keeping up with his endless food wishlist was never an easy task! Over time, food became more than just cooking. It became a way of creating memories, sharing happiness, and bringing people together. And that's how Pattanayak's Cuisine was born. Two siblings, Saini and Soham, decided to document their culinary adventures and share them with the world. From home-cooked comfort food and family recipes to hidden street-food gems, cozy cafés, and luxurious fine-dining restaurants, this blog is a collection of everything they explore, taste, and love. Because for us, food is not just about filling our stomachs — it's about stories, emotions, memories, and the people we share them with. Welcome to Pattanayak's Cuisine — a journey from our home kitchen to everywhere our taste buds take us.

We believe food is more than nourishment — it's memory, culture, and connection. Every dish we feature carries a moment, a conversation, or a discovery worth sharing.
