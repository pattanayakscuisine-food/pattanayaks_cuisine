বাংলাদেশী তেহারী

Bangladeshi Tehari - Bangladeshi Tehari is an aromatic, deeply flavorful, and slightly pungent one-pot meat and rice dish that represents a cornerstone of festive street food culture in Bangladesh, most notably in Old Dhaka (Puran Dhaka).

চিকেন ঝাল ফ্রাই

Chicken Jhal Fry - Chicken Jhal Fry is a fiery, tangy Indian stir-fry featuring tender chicken pieces tossed with crunchy bell peppers, onions, and tomatoes. Cooked over high heat with bold spices like cumin, green chilies, and garam masala, it delivers a spicy, mouthwatering kick.

See on Instagram
🍛

লক্ষী পুজোর ভোগ

Lakshmi Puja Bhog - Lakshmi Puja bhog consists of sacred, strictly vegetarian (niramish) food offerings made to the Goddess of wealth and prosperity. Once offered during prayers, it is distributed to devotees as prasad. In the frame the platter consists of Basanti Pulao, Khichuri, Beguni, Charchari, Fulkopi Bhapa, Paneer Posto, Bengal's Authentic Chutney, Payesh.

See on Instagram
🍛

চিকেন ভুনা খিচুরি

Chicken Bhuna Khichuri - Chicken Bhuna Khichuri is a rich, aromatic Bangladeshi one-pot meal made by cooking marinated chicken, rice, and roasted lentils together. The term bhuna refers to a slow-roasting technique where spices are caramelized, giving the dish a bold, dry, and pilaf-like texture rather than a mushy porridge consistency.

See on Instagram
🍛

বিরিয়ানি

Chicken Biriyani - Bengali-style chicken biryani , is a fragrant and refined rice dish featuring long-grain basmati rice, tender chicken, a subtle blend of spices, and its signature addition—a slow-cooked, golden-brown potato and a hard-boiled egg

Bengali Authentic Rainy Day Special Platter - When you are staying far away from your hometown, and you are a Bengali, and it's a rainy day. This is the platter you crave for. পদ:- ১.স্যালাড ২.পনীর ভাপা, ৩.সাদা ভাত, ৪.আলু-উচ্ছে ভাজা, ৫.ধনিয়া চিকেন

কাঁকড়ার ঝাল

Kakrar Jhal - Kakrar Jhal is a traditional, spicy Bengali crab curry hailing from West Bengal, India. It features fresh crab (Kakra) and golden potato chunks slow-cooked in a fiery, rich gravy infused with mustard oil, aromatic spices, and a finishing touch of pungent mustard paste.

See on Instagram

কড়াইশুঁটির কচুরি & আলুর দম

Koraishutir Kochuri and Aloor Dom is a classic Bengali food combination featuring a deep-fried, puffed flatbread stuffed with spiced green peas, served alongside a rich, spicy potato curry. It is a beloved breakfast and celebratory dish, particularly popular during the winter months when fresh ingredients are abundant.
